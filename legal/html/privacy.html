Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026 Effective date: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mend ("we", "us", "our", or the "Service") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using Mend, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

When you use Mend, you provide us with:

  • Account information — email address and password (the password is stored only as a salted hash on our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text).
  • Profile information — your name, age, and self-reported pre-injury fitness level.
  • Health information — a free-text description of your injury or condition, daily pain ratings (0–10), mood selections, hydration counts, exercise completion checkmarks, and any notes you write in the journal.
  • Subscription information — your subscription tier (when you subscribe through the App Store or Google Play).
  • Chat content — messages you send to the AI Physio chat feature.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information — basic data such as operating system, app version, and device language, used to deliver appropriate content.
  • Usage information — which screens you visit and which features you use. We do not collect precise location or browsing history.
  • Notification preferences — toggle states for in-app reminder categories (these stay on your device until you choose to sync).

1.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • Real-time location data
  • Camera, microphone, or photo library content
  • Contacts
  • Browsing history outside Mend
  • Biometric identifiers (face, fingerprint)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate your personalized rehabilitation plan using an AI model
  • Provide back-and-forth AI Physio chat grounded in your plan
  • Track your daily progress (exercise completion, pain, hydration, mood)
  • Sync your data across your devices when you sign in
  • Schedule local reminder notifications on your device
  • Manage your subscription
  • Improve the App
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We keep your account and synced data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile → Reset plan + Sign out, then contact us to remove the cloud copy), after which we delete your data within 30 days.

Local data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App or tap "Reset plan" + "Sign out".

Anonymized usage statistics may be retained for service improvement.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data:

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (we never see them in plain text)
  • Row-level security policies on our database ensure you can only access your own data
  • Sensitive credentials (AI API keys) stay on our servers and are never embedded in the App

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work hard to follow best practices.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate data (you can also edit your profile in the App)
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object / Restrict — object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent for processing where consent is the legal basis

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address in Section 12. We will respond within 30 days.

EU / EEA / UK residents (GDPR)

The legal bases for processing your data are: (a) performance of a contract (delivering the App's features you signed up for), (b) legitimate interest (improving the Service), and (c) consent (notifications, where you opted in).

California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

You have the right to know what categories of personal information we collect, the right to delete it, the right to correct it, and the right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Mend is intended for users aged 16 and older. If you are under 16, please do not use the App. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. AI Disclosure

Plans and chat responses are generated by a large language model. The model:

  • Receives only the information needed for the request (your injury description, your plan, and recent chat turns)
  • Does not store your data persistently on the model provider's side beyond their standard logging policies
  • Has been instructed to provide general rehabilitation guidance, not medical advice

AI output is not medical advice and may contain errors. Always consult a qualified clinician before acting on any plan or chat response — see Section 11.

9. International Data Transfers

Our servers and our service providers may be located outside your country of residence. Where applicable, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms approved by GDPR.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, by an in-app notice. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

11. Medical Disclaimer

Mend is a wellness and education tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a qualified clinician with any questions about a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of something you have read or generated in this App.
